# HearthFrame — Account Guidelines & Terms of Service

Welcome to **HearthFrame**, a lightweight, community-hosted digital photo frame web app built to turn old Android tablets, iPads, phones, and TVs into private family photo displays.

By creating an account on this HearthFrame server, you agree to the usage boundaries and guidelines below.

---

## 1. Server Account Cap (20 Accounts Total)

- This server is intentionally kept small and fast for friends and family, with a default hard limit of **20 total accounts** (configured via `MAX_ACCOUNTS`).
- Once the server reaches 20 accounts, new registrations are automatically paused until space becomes available.
- Please create **one account per household** rather than multiple duplicate accounts so friends and family have room to join.

---

## 2. Per-Account Photo Limit (100 Photos)

- Each standard user account may store up to **100 photos** at a time.
- Photos are automatically resized in your browser before uploading to keep storage lean and playback fast on older tablets and slow Wi-Fi networks.
- If you reach 100 photos, simply delete older photos from **Admin → Photos** to make room for new ones.

---

## 3. Fair-Use Signup & Security Limits

- To prevent automated abuse, registrations are limited to **3 new accounts per IP address per hour**.
- Your server host may optionally enable an **Invite Code** (`SIGNUP_INVITE_CODE`) to restrict new signups to invited friends and family.
- Screen pairing codes (QR / 6-character PIN) expire after **5 minutes**, are strictly single-use, and only grant **Frame Display** viewing access — never Admin access.

---

## 4. Privacy & Workspace Isolation

- Every account gets its own **isolated private workspace**. Your photos, captions, Display Profiles, and settings are never shared with or visible to other accounts.
- Signed photo links are bound to your workspace and expire automatically.
- The server owner's moderation view displays only basic account metadata (frame name, username, photo count, and creation date) and **never** displays your private photos.

---

## 5. Acceptable Use & Account Maintenance

- HearthFrame is intended for **personal, household, and family photo display only**. Do not upload illegal, harmful, copyrighted commercial distribution material, or abusive content.
- Because this is a shared, small-capacity server, the host reserves the right to remove **abusive, spam, or long-inactive accounts** (along with their stored photos) to keep the service running smoothly for everyone.

---

## 6. Keep Your Own Backups

- HearthFrame is designed as a **live digital photo display**, not a permanent cloud backup archive.
- Always keep original copies of your important photos backed up safely on your own devices or personal cloud storage.
